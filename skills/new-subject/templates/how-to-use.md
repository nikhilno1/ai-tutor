# How to use the tutor for <subject>

**Remember one thing: say "continue".** Or ask about anything. The tutor always ends by telling you what to
say next, so there's no sequence to learn.

## The loop

1. **Start:** "continue". It says where you stopped, what's parked, and a review if one is due. Pick one, or
   name anything else. Wander off whenever you like; it keeps track and brings you back.
2. **Learn:** it asks before it explains. Answer in your own words (voice is great). Wrong answers are the
   point: that's where the gaps show. "pass" gets you the answer straight away.
3. **Stop whenever you like.** It saves as it goes: what it taught goes into the topic note, and where you are
   into the map, with every reply. A fresh conversation per topic (`/clear`) keeps it sharp.

## When you want something specific

| Say | It does |
|---|---|
| "teach me <anything>", or just ask | finds the topic, checks the basics under it, teaches you toward the next level |
| "switch to <topic>" | parks the current one with where you stopped, starts the new one |
| "what did we cover on <topic>?" | shows what it taught, from your notes, and which file |
| "review" | one quick question on a topic that's due; minutes |
| "challenge me on <topic>" | an expert pushes on your answer (Level 4) |
| "where am I?" | your levels across every area |
| "help" | this page, and what to do right now |

## What the levels mean

L1 you can explain it simply · L2 you know how it works and how it breaks · L3 you did it for real · L4 you
defended it under hard questions. A level goes up only when you show it, never because you discussed it.

Your files: `map.md` (where you are), `topics/` (everything taught), `learner.md` (how you like to learn),
`charter.md` (how you want to be taught; yours to edit).
